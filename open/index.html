🧧 You got a red envelope

Someone sent you a red envelope (紅包) in Messages. To open it and read the greeting, install Red Envelope from the App Store on your iPhone, then tap the envelope in Messages again.

Any gift of money is sent separately with Apple Cash, so check your Messages for it.

有人在「訊息」傳給你一個紅包。請在 App Store 安裝 Red Envelope，再點一下訊息中的紅包即可打開。
