🧧 Red Envelope

Red Envelope is an iMessage app for sending a red envelope (紅包) with a greeting in Chinese and English. Put money in it, which you send yourself with Apple Cash, or a digital lucky charm (電子幸運符): a picture with a blessing, and no money.

How to send one

  1. Open a conversation in Messages.
  2. Tap +, then Red Envelope.
  3. Choose Money or Digital Lucky Charm. Tap Customize… to pick the occasion, style and greeting.
  4. With money: send it with Apple Cash, then come back and add your envelope. With a digital lucky charm: tap Add to Message.
  5. Tap Send.

To practise without sending anything, open the Red Envelope app from your Home Screen.

Questions

Does the envelope contain money? No. The money is sent separately with Apple Cash. Red Envelope can't send money or check that it was sent.

What's a digital lucky charm? A talisman picture with the occasion's character and a four-character blessing. It has no money value. The person who opens it can add it to Apple Wallet.

Can I know when my envelope is opened? Yes, if you want. In the Red Envelope app, turn on "When my envelope is opened", then turn on "Tell me when it's opened" when you customize an envelope. The person you send it to is told before they open it. See the privacy policy for what this sends.

Can it remind me about Lunar New Year? Yes. Turn on "Lunar New Year reminder" in the Red Envelope app for a reminder three days before.

Where does it work? Apple Cash is available in the United States, so Red Envelope is too.

The person I sent it to sees a link. They need Red Envelope installed to open the envelope.
