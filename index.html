🧧 Red Envelope

Red Envelope is an iMessage app for sending a red envelope (紅包) with a greeting in Chinese and English. You send the money yourself with Apple Cash; the envelope carries the greeting.

How to send one

  1. Open a conversation in Messages.
  2. Tap +, then Red Envelope.
  3. Pick an amount, an occasion and a design.
  4. Send the money with Apple Cash, then come back and add your envelope.

To practise without sending anything, open the Red Envelope app from your Home Screen.

Questions

Does the envelope contain money? No. The money is sent separately with Apple Cash. Red Envelope can't send money or check that it was sent.

Where does it work? Apple Cash is available in the United States, so Red Envelope is too.

The person I sent it to sees a link. They need Red Envelope installed to open the envelope.
