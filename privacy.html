Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Red Envelope collects no data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it doesn't send anything to a server run by us.

What stays on your device

The app saves your language choice and an envelope you haven't finished, so you can pick up where you left off. These stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

What you send

An envelope you send contains the greeting, occasion and design. It doesn't contain the amount. It travels through Apple's Messages service like any other message, between you and the people in the conversation.

Money

Red Envelope never sees or handles money or payment details. You send money with Apple Cash, which is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

This website

If someone opens an envelope without the app installed, their browser loads a page on this site, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits as described in its privacy statement. We don't receive or keep those logs.

Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue.
